# Game Concept: Hostile World

*Created: 26 April 2026*
*Status: Draft*

---

## Elevator Pitch

> A black ops mercenary — the only person immune to a planet-wide alien virus — escapes a collapsing high-security mountain prison during an infection outbreak and descends into an open world of infected towns, resistance camps, and alien hives to uncover the truth about the invasion and find a way to stop it.

## Core Fantasy

You are the only person alive who is immune to the alien virus. Every location you enter is dangerous, every survivor you meet is a potential ally or victim, and every step deeper into the infection brings you closer to the terrifying truth about why you survived.

---

## Unique Hook

The alien infection physically transforms the world in real time — zones evolve, spread, and collapse around the player. This is not a static open world. The world is the conspiracy.

*Passes the "and also" test: It's like a survival RPG where you're uncovering an alien conspiracy, AND ALSO every area around you is actively mutating as you play.*

## Player Experience Analysis (MDA Framework)

### Target Aesthetics

| Aesthetic | Priority | How We Deliver It |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **Sensation** | 2 | Cinematic third-person camera, environmental audio, visceral impact feedback |
| **Fantasy** | 3 | You are a uniquely immune mercenary — dangerous and capable |
| **Narrative** | 1 | Player-driven story uncovered through exploration and survivor choices |
| **Challenge** | 2 | Lethal combat, scarce resources, adaptive enemy AI |
| **Fellowship** | 4 | Survivor alliances, camp relationships, faction choices |
| **Discovery** | 1 | Lore, hidden areas, alien research, conspiracy clues |
| **Expression** | N/A | Not a creative-expression game |
| **Submission** | N/A | High-tension, not relaxing |

### Key Dynamics

- Players will explore every corner before moving on, driven by FOMO about missed clues
- Players will experiment with survivor alliance choices and feel the consequences
- Players will share discoveries and theories with each other
- Players will feel the world changing around them and adapt routes accordingly

### Core Mechanics

1. **Dangerous Exploration** — traverse hostile zones with infected patrols, environmental hazards, and resource scarcity. Every zone is alive and mutating.
2. **Tactical Combat** — third-person combat with lethal damage, stealth, and adaptive alien enemy AI. The player is skilled, not overpowered.
3. **Conspiracy Investigation** — gather intel from survivors, environments, and alien research. Piece together the truth through earned discovery.
4. **Survivor Alliance System** — build trust with factions and individuals. Choose who to help, who to trade with, who to save. Factions respond to your reputation and choices.
5. **Infection Spreading Mechanic** — a dynamic system where alien infection zones evolve in real time. The world mutates around the player, creating new threats and new opportunities.

## Player Motivation Profile

### Primary Psychological Needs Served

| Need | How This Game Satisfies It | Strength |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **Autonomy** | Player chooses where to explore, who to help, what intel to pursue | Core |
| **Competence** | Mastery of combat, stealth, resource management, and investigation | Core |
| **Relatedness** | Survivor alliances, faction relationships, earned trust | Supporting |

### Player Type Appeal

- [x] **Achievers** — faction progression, conspiracy completion, survival milestones
- [x] **Explorers** — lore hunting, secret zones, conspiracy unraveling (primary)
- [ ] **Socializers** — not a multiplayer game
- [ ] **Killers/Competitors** — not a PvP game

### Flow State Design

- **Onboarding curve**: First 10 minutes in the prison — teach movement, stealth, and survival before the infection hits
- **Difficulty scaling**: Zones have dynamic threat levels. Player skill grows through practice, not stat inflation.
- **Feedback clarity**: Combat mastery shows through enemy behavior changes; investigation mastery shows through faster revelations
- **Recovery from failure**: Death is meaningful — load from checkpoint, but lose progress on that run's choices

## Core Loop

### Moment-to-Moment (30 seconds)

Traverse a hostile zone — read environmental cues, manage stamina and visibility, avoid or eliminate patrols, locate key items and intel. The core 30-second action is movement through danger.

### Short-Term (5-15 minutes)

Navigate between points of interest within a zone — encounter survivors, scavenge for resources, investigate intel, survive encounters. "One more clue" psychology through branching investigation paths.

### Session-Level (60-120 minutes)

A complete session: enter a zone or region, complete an objective or investigation arc, extract and process discoveries, return to camp or push deeper. Ends with a natural save point AND an open thread that pulls the player back.

### Long-Term Progression

Three parallel progression tracks:
1. **Story** — conspiracy revelation, main story advancement
2. **Factions** — survivor alliances and faction reputation
3. **Player Skill** — mastery growth through play, not stat inflation

The game is done when the conspiracy is fully understood and resolved.

### Retention Hooks

- **Curiosity**: Where did the infection come from? Who else survived? What's in the next zone?
- **Investment**: Faction relationships, discovered intel, survivor lives
- **Mastery**: Cleaner stealth kills, faster investigation reads, harder survival runs

## Game Pillars

### Pillar 1: Hostile World
The world is alive and actively transforming — the mountain prison is just the starting point. As the protagonist descends into an open world of towns, villages, and camps, the alien infection spreads everywhere and forces the player to adapt constantly.

*Design test*: If a feature makes the world feel static or safe as the player progresses, this pillar says cut it.

### Pillar 2: Earned Discovery
Every clue, location, and revelation is found through exploration and survivor interaction — never handed to the player through automatic cutscenes or journal dumps.

*Design test*: If a feature reveals story automatically without player interaction, this pillar says it needs a mechanic gate.

### Pillar 3: Tense Survival
Resources are scarce and every choice has weight — the player feels the pressure of being uniquely immune but never safe.

*Design test*: If a feature removes survival tension, this pillar says it needs risk reintroduced.

### Anti-Pillars (What This Game Is NOT)

- **NOT a power fantasy**: The player is capable and dangerous, but the world is deadlier. Dominating enemies easily undermines survival tension.
- **NOT infection as a horror gimmick**: The alien infection has real story consequences and world-altering mechanics — not just jump scares.
- **NOT a "chosen one" narrative**: The immune protagonist's immunity has a logical cause through investigation, not destiny or prophecy.

## Risks and Open Questions

### Design Risks

- The core loop may not sustain sessions if investigation pacing is inconsistent
- Player motivation after the main conspiracy resolves needs a strong faction system to carry post-game
- The immune protagonist's power level must be carefully calibrated — too powerful and survival tension dies

### Technical Risks

- Dynamic world transformation system has no proven UE5 pipeline — requires aggressive prototyping
- Alien creature AI needs to feel adaptive and unpredictable — needs R&D time
- Open world infection spread may cause performance issues on mid-range hardware

### Market Risks

- Survival RPG market is competitive but underserved for conspiracy-first titles
- UE5 scope for solo dev is aggressive — scope management is the single biggest risk
- First-time developer — no existing audience to leverage

### Scope Risks

- Open world content volume exceeds solo dev capacity at full scope
- Infection spread system is technically ambitious — must be prototyped early
- Dynamic zones may require significantly more art content than static alternatives

### Open Questions

- How exactly does the immune protagonist's immunity work? (Proto: investigation reveals fragments)
- What is the alien intelligence's goal? (Proto: conspiracy investigation arc)
- Can the infection be reversed? (Proto: player choice in ending)

## MVP Definition

**Core hypothesis**: Players find the dangerous exploration + conspiracy investigation loop engaging for 60+ minute sessions when the world feels alive and the story rewards curiosity.

**Required for MVP**:

1. One vertical zone with dynamic infection spread
2. Core exploration and investigation mechanics
3. Three survivor NPCs with dialogue and faction consequences
4. Combat that feels lethal and intentional
5. One conspiracy thread with a reveal

**Explicitly NOT in MVP**:

- Full open world — one zone only
- Full faction system — three NPCs, no reputation graph
- Full infection spread — one zone, scripted events
- Full alien creature roster — one enemy type

### Scope Tiers

| Tier | Content | Features | Timeline |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **MVP** | One vertical zone | Core loop only | 3-4 months |
| **Vertical Slice** | One complete zone + camp hub | Core + factions + alien threat | 5-6 months |
| **Alpha** | Mountain + 2 open regions | All features, rough | 10-12 months |
| **Full Vision** | All regions + all systems | Polished, complete | 18-24 months |
